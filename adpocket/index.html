AdPocket: Yandex Advertising Network statistics on your phone

A pocket statistics client for the Yandex Advertising Network.

Google PlayApp Store

What AdPocket is

AdPocket puts the Yandex Advertising Network (YAN) partner dashboard in your pocket. Open the app and see in seconds how your sites and apps are earning today, spot trends, build any report the web interface can, and get a summary every morning – all without a laptop. AdPocket is an independent app for publishers and app developers; it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Yandex.

Overview at a glance

  • Today's revenue with the change versus yesterday.
  • Yesterday, the last 7 days, this month and last month, each with its comparison.
  • A 30-day revenue and impressions chart.
  • eCPM, fill rate and CTR for the period you pick.
  • Top apps and sites by revenue, one tap away from a detailed report.

Full YAN reports

Main, Mobile Mediation, SSP and DSP statistics are all there. Pick a preset period or any custom date range, then use the same metrics, groupings (by day, week, month, geography, app, block, OS, browser and more) and filters as on partner.yandex.ru. Results come as totals, a sortable table or a chart, and you can save presets and export to CSV.

Widget, summaries and alerts

A home-screen widget shows today's revenue, the trend and a refresh button. Daily and monthly summaries arrive at the time you choose, and alerts tell you when revenue drops or impressions stop. All of them are generated on your device, with no push servers.

Accounts and privacy

Keep several YAN accounts side by side. Tokens are stored in the system secure store (Android Keystore or iOS Keychain), and an optional app lock uses your fingerprint, face or device PIN. AdPocket has no server of its own, no analytics and no ads: your OAuth token and your statistics stay on your device and are sent only to Yandex's API. The interface is in Russian and English, with currency and VAT settings and light and dark themes.

Who it is for

Website owners and app developers who earn with the Yandex Advertising Network and want to check revenue and reports from their phone, not only from the web interface at a computer.

Frequently asked questions

Is AdPocket an official Yandex app?

No. AdPocket is an independent application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Yandex. Yandex and Yandex Advertising Network are trademarks of Yandex LLC.

How do I sign in?

Open partner.yandex.ru, press the "API" button on the right panel, choose "Get OAuth token for the Statistics API" and paste the token into the app. The app shows these steps on its first screen.

Where is my data stored?

On your device only. The OAuth token is kept in the system secure store and statistics are requested directly from Yandex's API. AdPocket has no server and sends nothing to the developer.

Does AdPocket show ads?

No. AdPocket contains no ads, no analytics and no trackers.
