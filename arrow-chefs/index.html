What Arrow Chefs is

Arrow Chefs is a calm puzzle about a busy kitchen. You lay arrow tiles on the floor; the cooks walk straight ahead and turn wherever an arrow points, from the crate to the oven, past the chopping board and out through the serving window. When every order is served, the kitchen is solved. Every run is exactly the same, so you can stop, fix one arrow and try again.

Three kitchens and a breakfast every day

  • Corner Bakery – dough, the oven and your first routes.
  • Salad Bar – the chopping board and orders with two items.
  • Night Diner – rush kitchens with timed orders and two cooks at once.
  • Breakfast of the day – a fresh little kitchen every morning with eggs, bacon and pancakes, and your morning streak.

Cooks that bend the rules

Pip carries one thing at a time, the Juggler carries two, and the Runner moves two tiles per step. Stars reward clever routes: one for serving every order, two for using no more arrows than par, three for also finishing within par steps.

Leaderboards from day one

Today's board ranks the fewest arrows, then the fewest steps, then the fastest time on the daily breakfast; the all-time board counts your stars. You play under a made-up chef name: no account, no sign-up.

Gentle ads, no purchases

Arrow Chefs is free and supported by ads, with no in-app purchases. There are no ads in your first three sessions; after that, a short ad may appear only after a finished level, never while you play. Three hints a day are free. The game works offline in 19 languages, on phones, tablets and foldables.

Frequently asked questions

Is Arrow Chefs free?

Yes. It is free and supported by ads; there are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Does it need the internet?

No. Every level and the daily breakfast work offline; only the leaderboards and ads need a connection.

Do I need an account?

No. The leaderboards use an anonymous sign-in and a made-up chef name.

Which languages does it support?

19 languages, including English, Russian, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Turkish, Ukrainian, Armenian, Georgian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese.

Where can I download it?

Arrow Chefs is available on Google Play now. The App Store and RuStore versions are coming soon.
