What BoomErange is

BoomErange is the app of Boomerang, the clothing store in Gyumri, Armenia. It is made for the shop's customers: see your bonus points and what is left to pay, keep track of every purchase, and browse the shop's newest looks straight from its Instagram. The shop's photos and contacts are open to everyone; your bonuses and purchases appear after you sign in.

Home

  • Your bonus points and current balance at a glance.
  • When your points expire, with a clear countdown.
  • The shop's latest Instagram posts: swipe through photos, watch videos and zoom into every detail.

Purchases

  • Every purchase with its date, item and colour.
  • The total, paid and remaining amount for each purchase.
  • Your balance after each purchase and the bonuses you used.

Bonus points

You earn points with every purchase, and one point equals one dram. Use points to pay part of a purchase or save them up for later; the app shows when points were earned and when they expire, so none of them are lost by surprise.

Your account

Sign in with the phone number you gave the shop, or with Google on Android. The app shows your personal QR code to show in the shop and can notify you about new purchases, bonuses and new arrivals. BoomErange is available in Armenian, Russian and English, with light and dark themes. It keeps only what the service needs – your phone number and name, your purchases and bonus points – and nothing is sold.

Permissions and privacy

Notifications are sent only if you allow them, and the app asks for your photos only when you choose a profile picture yourself. The shop's photos and videos are loaded from Instagram. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Profile → Delete My Account, which removes your sign-in, your bonuses and your notification token. BoomErange is available on Google Play and the App Store.
