What Captain Giraffe is

Captain Giraffe is an educational game for babies and toddlers. The captain tows six worlds behind his boat: Safari, Farm, Vehicles, Sea, Fruit and Shapes. Swipe the rope, pick a world and play games made for the smallest hands – no reading, no timers and no wrong answers that sting. It is calm enough for a one-year-old and interesting enough for a four-year-old.

Eight gentle games

  • Puzzle – put three pieces together to reveal the character.
  • Find the pairs – flip cards and match the same animal.
  • Big & small – sort three sizes onto their shadows.
  • Whose name? – hear a name and find the character; a hint plays its real sound.
  • Peekaboo – tap a bush and see who jumps out.
  • Match – drag ten characters onto their shadows.
  • Who lives here? – sort animals and vehicles into the right world.
  • Feed me – hand fruit to a hungry animal and fill its tummy.

Made for ages 1–4

Cards are large and drop zones are generous, and a gentle wobble hints at the answer when a child pauses. Mistakes simply bounce back, and every round ends with a cheer as the characters board the boat. Names are spoken in English or Russian, and the animal and vehicle sounds are real recordings. Calm music, sea sounds and light vibration can each be switched off.

Grown-ups stay in control

Settings and the exit are behind a press-and-hold, so little fingers cannot leave the game or change anything by accident. Sound, music, vibration, voice and language are in one parent panel. The game works fully offline and needs no account or sign-up.

Private and ad-free

Captain Giraffe has no ads, no in-app purchases, no chat and no external links for a child to tap. It makes no network requests, requests no device permissions and collects no personal data; settings and progress stay on the device. It is designed for phones and tablets in landscape, and it is meant to be played together with a parent.

Frequently asked questions

What age is Captain Giraffe for?

It is made for children aged 1 to 4 and is meant to be played together with a parent.

Does Captain Giraffe have ads or in-app purchases?

No. The game has no ads and no purchases, and it collects no personal data.

Does it need the internet?

No. Captain Giraffe works fully offline, makes no network requests and asks for no device permissions.

Which languages does it speak?

Character names are spoken in English or Russian; you can switch the language in the parent panel.
