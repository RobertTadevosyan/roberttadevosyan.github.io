Tile Titans: a merge puzzle with numbered tiles

Merge matching tiles into bigger ones in a relaxed number puzzle.

Google Play

What Tile Titans is

Tile Titans is a number tile game built on the slide-and-merge idea that 2048 made famous. Slide the numbered tiles across the grid; when two tiles with the same value meet, they merge into one bigger tile. Plan your swipes, keep the board under control and climb towards bigger numbers and a new best score. It works as a relaxing pastime for a short break and as a proper brain workout when you want to think hard.

Four game modes

Tile Titans offers four modes – Timed, Zen, Hardcore and AI Challenge – so the same simple rules can be played calmly or as a real test of strategy. Your moves, merges and efficiency are tracked, so you can see how each game went and what to improve next time.

Features

  • Themes including Classic, Dark, Neon and Ocean.
  • Smart hints and an auto-play option driven by a simple AI.
  • A global leaderboard to compare your best score with players worldwide.
  • Sound effects you can switch on or off.
  • A layout made for both phones and tablets.

Privacy

The only permission Tile Titans asks for is internet access, used for ads and the leaderboard. It never asks for your contacts, camera, microphone, location, photos or files. Your current board, best scores, per-mode records and settings are stored on your device.

Who it is for

Tile Titans suits casual players who want a short brain break as well as puzzle fans who like to plan several moves ahead. You do not need an account and you never type in a name or e-mail. The game plays fully offline; when you are online it signs in anonymously so that your high score can appear on the leaderboard, which shows only the score, your platform and your device model.

Tile Titans is free and paid for by ads, with no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. Your first sessions are ad-free, and a full-screen ad never appears in the middle of a move.
